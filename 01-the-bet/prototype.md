# The Prototype Bet

## What I Built
<!-- One sentence: what does this prototype demonstrate? -->
This prototype demonstrates a single-screen pre-RFP sales intelligence dashboard that automatically scans unformatted municipal records, flags early buying signals, and generates instant draft outreach to key decision-makers.

## AI Value Archetype
<!-- Automator / Copilot / Oracle / Creator / Orchestrator -->
Oracle: "Surfaces hidden signals" — it is ingesting massive volumes of unstructured local government data (meeting transcripts, council minutes, budgets) to detect obscure, early buying intent 6–12 months before an RFP exists.

## The Bet in One Sentence
<!-- What you're building, for whom, why now -->
Building an AI-driven pre-RFP monitoring tool for enterprise sales teams targeting local government will allow them to engage decision-makers 6–12 months before public tenders drop, giving them an unfair advantage over competitors who rely solely on active bid boards.

## Kill Criteria
<!-- When would you stop? What evidence would kill this bet? -->
Irrelevant Signals: If more than 30% of AI-flagged council minutes or transcript excerpts are deemed false positives by sales users.

Zero Outreach Conversion: If cold outreach generated via pre-RFP signals results in a <5% response rate from municipal directors after 60 days of testing.

Data Ingestion Friction: If scaling document extraction across fragmented municipal sources requires prohibitive human manual effort or excessive LLM compute costs per lead generated.
